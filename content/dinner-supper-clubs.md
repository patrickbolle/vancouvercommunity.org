# 🍽️ Dinner / Supper Clubs

## DayOfUs - Dinner with Strangers
- **What:** Weekly curated dinners — you + 5 strangers at a surprise restaurant. Algorithm-matched based on interests, casual, low-pressure
- **When:** Weekly
- **Find it:** [dayofus.com/events/vancouver](https://www.dayofus.com/events/vancouver)

## Timeleft
- **What:** International app, weekly dinners with strangers. Algorithm-matched, launched in Vancouver May 2024
- **When:** Weekly
- **Find it:** [timeleft.com](https://timeleft.com/)

## Swallow Tail Pop-Up Dining
- **What:** Underground dining events, secret supper clubs, long table dinners. Creative, immersive, "weird wonderful brains"
- **Find it:** [swallowtail.ca/pop-dining](https://swallowtail.ca/pop-dining)

## A Moveable Feast Supper Club
- **What:** French wine bar inspired dinners. "Between strangers and soon-to-be friends"
- **Find it:** [Eventbrite](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/a-moveable-feast-supper-club-tickets-1918910465859)

## The Artisans supper Club
- **What:** Secret supper clubs with curated fine dining set up and place to meet new people and make friends. Engage in activities as cake painting and games
- **Find it:** [instagram.com/theartisanssupperclub](https://www.instagram.com/theartisanssupperclub/)

## Vegan Meetup Vancouver
- **What:** Vancouver's oldest veg social group on Meetup, running since 2002. Shared vegan dinners at local restaurants, sometimes with special menus made just for the group. Veg-curious people welcome
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/vegvan](https://www.meetup.com/vegvan/)

## Vancouver Dinner Club
- **What:** Small dinners with 4 to 6 new people at a restaurant downtown, in Yaletown or on the east side. For people in their 20s to 40s who want real friends, not a big crowd. Book a ticket through Groupvibe, and the restaurant is shared the night before
- **When:** Saturdays 7pm
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/vancouver-dinner-club](https://www.meetup.com/vancouver-dinner-club/)

## Dim Sum Queen
- **What:** Group dim sum outings run by a host who grew up going to dim sum with her grandma. Brunches and early dinners at Chinese restaurants in Vancouver, Richmond and Burnaby, about once a month. First-timers welcome. Bring cash, as some places don't split bills
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/dim-sum-queen](https://www.meetup.com/dim-sum-queen/)
