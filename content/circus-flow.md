# 🤹 Circus, Juggling & Flow Arts

## Open Acro Jam at STRETCH
- **What:** Weekly acro yoga jam with "no teacher, no set plan: mats, open space, and a room full of people who love to fly." All levels, and you can fly, base or spot
- **When:** Wednesdays 5:30–8pm
- **Cost:** By donation
- **Where:** STRETCH, 180 E Pender St, Chinatown
- **Find it:** [stretchvancouver.com](https://www.stretchvancouver.com/open-acro-jam-wednesdays/)

## Acro Umbrella
- **What:** Vancouver acro community with acro yoga classes, hand balancing and beginner-friendly jams. "Low-pressure spaces to move, meet people, and enjoy acro together"
- **When:** Jams every other Sunday
- **Cost:** Jams by donation
- **Where:** Aché, 341 E Broadway, and other studios
- **Find it:** [acroumbrella.com](https://acroumbrella.com/)

## Rising Sun AcroYoga
- **What:** Beginner-friendly acro yoga classes. No partner and no experience needed, and your first class is free
- **Where:** 341 E Broadway, Mount Pleasant
- **Find it:** [risingsunacroyoga.com](https://www.risingsunacroyoga.com/)

## CircusWest
- **What:** "Vancouver's one-stop hub for circus arts." Six-week adult and teen classes in aerial silks, trapeze, juggling, handstands, partner acro and Cyr wheel
- **Where:** Garden Auditorium, PNE Grounds, Hastings-Sunrise
- **Find it:** [circuswest.com](https://www.circuswest.com/)

## Vancouver Circus School
- **What:** Circus school with adult classes in silks, trapeze, juggling, handstands and "Everything Circus." Open training lets you drop in and practise your aerial, acro or prop skills
- **Where:** 810 Quayside Dr, New Westminster
- **Find it:** [vancouvercircusschool.ca](https://vancouvercircusschool.ca/classes-drop-ins/recreational-classes/)
- **Notes:** Free Circus Days on most statutory holidays

## Radiant Life Hoops
- **What:** Adult hula hoop classes for fun, fitness "and giggles," from first tricks to intermediate flow
- **Where:** Showpony Studio, 2280 E Hastings St
- **Find it:** [radiantlifehoops.com](https://radiantlifehoops.com/hoop-classes-and-lessons/adult-hoop-classes-hoop-classes-and-lessons/adult-hula-hoop-classes/)
