# 🎿 Ski & Snowboard

## Vancouver Ski & Snowboard Crew
- **What:** Meetups at Grouse, Cypress and Seymour, regular Whistler trips and the odd backcountry outing. Pre-season pub nights to find ski buddies
- **Find it:** [meetup.com/rumblefish-sports](https://www.meetup.com/rumblefish-sports/)

## UBC Ski & Board Club
- **What:** UBC student club for anyone stoked on skiing and snowboarding. Mountain trips, Girls Ride Days, park sessions and lots of socials. No experience needed, any year or program
- **Where:** UBC
- **Find it:** [amsclubs.ca/ski-and-board](https://amsclubs.ca/ski-and-board/)

## Nordic Racers Ski Club
- **What:** Cross-country ski club for recreational skiers and racers alike. You don't need to race to join. Group ski outings, clinics, socials and an active para nordic program
- **Where:** Hollyburn (Cypress Mountain) and Whistler Olympic Park
- **Find it:** [nordicracers.ca](https://www.nordicracers.ca/)

## Nordic Wrecks (Valley Outdoor Association)
- **What:** Cross-country ski and snowshoe trips, now the Nordic group of the Valley Outdoor Association. Monthly socials with trip sign-ups
- **Where:** Pinetree Community Centre, Coquitlam
- **Find it:** [nordicwrecks.org](https://nordicwrecks.org/)

## North Shore Hikers
- **What:** Volunteer-run club since 1958. Backcountry skiing and snowshoeing in winter, led by experienced members in small groups. Two free trial events
- **Find it:** [northshorehikers.org](https://northshorehikers.org/)

## Vancouver Adaptive Snow Sports (VASS)
- **What:** Non-profit giving ski, sit-ski and snowboard lessons to people living with a physical or cognitive disability. VASS trains its volunteer instructors
- **Where:** Grouse, Cypress and Seymour
- **Find it:** [vass.ca](https://vass.ca/)

## Venues & Resources

## Cypress Mountain
- **What:** Downhill skiing and boarding for all levels, plus cross-country and snowshoe trails on Hollyburn Ridge. Adult lessons and rentals
- **Find it:** [cypressmountain.com](https://www.cypressmountain.com/)

## Grouse Mountain
- **What:** Ski and snowboard programs for every age and ability
- **Find it:** [grousemountain.com](https://www.grousemountain.com/)

## Mt Seymour
- **What:** Local hill with lessons, multi-day programs and snowshoeing
- **Where:** North Vancouver
- **Find it:** [mtseymour.ca](https://mtseymour.ca/)
