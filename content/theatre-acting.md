# 🎬 Theatre & Acting

## Metro Theatre
- **What:** Non-profit community theatre in an old Marpole movie house. Volunteer casts put on comedies, mysteries, musicals and a British panto every holiday season
- **Where:** 1370 SW Marine Drive, Marpole
- **Find it:** [metrotheatre.com](https://metrotheatre.com/)
- **Notes:** Auditions are posted at [metrotheatre.com/auditions](https://metrotheatre.com/auditions/)

## United Players of Vancouver
- **What:** "A community theatre company renowned for its exciting and demanding repertoire of productions." Holds auditions and welcomes volunteers
- **Where:** Jericho Arts Centre, Jericho Beach
- **Find it:** [unitedplayers.com](https://unitedplayers.com/)

## Vagabond Players
- **What:** Community theatre in a little playhouse in Queen's Park. Four shows a season, open auditions and a friendly volunteer crew
- **Where:** Bernie Legge Theatre, Queen's Park, New Westminster
- **Find it:** [vagabondplayers.ca](https://www.vagabondplayers.ca/)

## Deep Cove Stage Society
- **What:** Community theatre "entertaining Deep Cove since 1974." All cast and crew roles are volunteer, and membership is free
- **Where:** Deep Cove Shaw Theatre, 4360 Gallant Ave, North Vancouver
- **Find it:** [deepcovestage.com](https://deepcovestage.com/)

## White Rock Players' Club
- **What:** "A volunteer organization, non-profit dedicated to Community Theatre." Actors, set builders, painters and ushers all welcome
- **Where:** Oceana PARC Playhouse, 1532 Johnston Rd, White Rock
- **Find it:** [whiterockplayers.ca](https://www.whiterockplayers.ca/)

## Royal City Musical Theatre
- **What:** Volunteer-led musical theatre company that brings pros, emerging talent and volunteers together for one big spring musical a year
- **Where:** Massey Theatre, New Westminster
- **Find it:** [royalcitymusicaltheatre.com](https://royalcitymusicaltheatre.com/)

## The Broadway Chorus
- **What:** "Vancouver's most irreverent community musical theatre group." Weekly rehearsals and two fully staged shows a year. You don't need to read music
- **Where:** Rehearsals Tuesday evenings in Vancouver
- **Find it:** [thebroadwaychorus.com](https://www.thebroadwaychorus.com/join)

## Theatre Terrific
- **What:** Inclusive theatre company for artists of all abilities. Classes like Acting for All Abilities welcome everyone "from seasoned pros to those stepping onto the stage for the first time"
- **Where:** Woodward's Building, 111 W Hastings St
- **Find it:** [theatreterrific.ca](https://www.theatreterrific.ca/)
- **Notes:** Pay-what-you-can option for classes

## Andi Matheny Acting Studios
- **What:** Evening acting classes for adults: scene study, character work, improv warm-ups and self-tapes. Drop-ins welcome when space allows
- **Where:** South Granville
- **Find it:** [andimathenyactingstudios.com](https://www.andimathenyactingstudios.com/vancouver)
